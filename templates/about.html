CORE CAPABILITIES

Built for Modern Campus Needs

Engineered from the ground up to solve real day-to-day challenges faced by academic registrars and departments.

Comprehensive Student Records

Maintain detailed biographical records, enrollment histories, guardian emergency contacts, and personalized notes for each student in one single source of truth.

GPA & Performance Insights

Track cumulative GPAs, monitor semester-by-semester trends, and identify students who may require academic support or merit scholarship recognition.

Instant Search & Smart Filters

Instantly search by name, student ID, or email, and slice records by academic department or enrollment status with zero latency thanks to client-side filtering.

Multi-Program Organization

Manage diverse academic programs ranging from BCA and BSc IT to B.Tech, MCA, Data Science, and specialized vocational certificates with tailored attributes.

Reliable & Safe Architecture

ACID-compliant SQLite relational database engine, input sanitization, and structured transactional integrity prevent record loss and corruption.

Fully Responsive Experience

Optimized for desktops, tablets, and smartphones, ensuring staff and faculty can access or update information whether at their desk or on campus grounds.
